About

I am a Lecturer in the Department of Electrical and Electronic Engineering at East Delta University, Chattogram, Bangladesh. I hold a BSc in Electrical and Electronic Engineering from the Bangladesh University of Engineering and Technology (BUET), where I majored in communication and signal processing, and I am currently pursuing my MSc there.

My research focuses on brain-computer interfaces and neural decoding, in particular decoding inner (imagined) speech from EEG using deep learning, with an emphasis on generalizing across subjects. I am interested in neural engineering more broadly: decoding and processing of neural signals, neuroprosthetics and assistive devices, and neurostimulation. My goal is to build technology that restores lost function for people with paralysis and neurological conditions.

I enjoy work that combines hardware with software and AI, and I am seeking to pursue a PhD in neural engineering starting in Fall 2027.

Research

Research Focus

  1. Cross-Subject Generalization of Inner Speech Decoding Using Deep Learning

    MSc Thesis, BUET (ongoing)

    This work studies how imagined (inner) speech from a closed vocabulary can be decoded from EEG, and how decoders can generalize to new subjects whose brain signals differ from those seen during training. I use deep learning together with domain adaptation and few-shot calibration so that a decoder needs only a small amount of data from a new user. I also examine how much of a decoder's performance reflects genuine neural activity rather than artifacts or recording confounds.

  2. Research Interests

    Brain-computer interfaces; neural decoding of speech and movement; invasive and non-invasive neural signal processing; neuroprosthetics and assistive robotics; neurostimulation for neurological conditions.
